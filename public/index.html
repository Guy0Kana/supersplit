How this is calculated

Each signal is scaled against its own 90th percentile over the previous 30 days, then weighted. Move the sliders to see how the score responds.

score = 100 × Σ weight × min(1, signal ÷ p90)

Services

How an engagement runs

Testing starts only after a signed scope and written authorisation.

  1. ScopeWe agree targets, dates and exclusions in writing.
  2. AuthoriseYou sign the rules of engagement.
  3. TestWork happens inside the agreed window.
  4. ReportFindings by severity, with evidence and fixes.
  5. RetestWe confirm the fixes hold.
